Five complete menus that account for the occasion, dietary constraints, the season and the harmony of starter, main and dessert. For example: morel tartines → poule au pot → tarte tatin.

03Ingredient Substitutions

Missing an ingredient? The AI suggests a replacement and its ratio — labneh → Greek yoghurt + salt, butter → oil (0.8:1). The book already contains many; the app generates more.

"I don't have labneh — what can I use instead?"

Stories

Four kitchens, one table. The La Brélie recipes come from notebooks, handwritten cards, gestures passed down and friendships — Mami Belfort, Bonne Maman, Zina, Thierry and Arthur.

Mami Belfort

The worn notebook

A notebook with a worn cover, quantities noted "by eye". We measured, tasted, started over.

Bonne Maman

Four cuisines, one table

The South of France, Brittany, Paris and the Maghreb — recipes that held a table of fifteen without ever faltering.

Zina

A Lebanese friendship

These recipes don't come from a trip. They come from a friendship — Zina's — and from a love for her country.

Thierry & Arthur

Why this book exists

A love of cooking. And because an unwritten recipe disappears. 365 recipes, tested twice, sometimes ten times.
