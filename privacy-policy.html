Last updated: 01/10/2026

This is a first-draft placeholder, not a finished legal document. Because this policy covers real personal data (newsletter sign-ups), it should be reviewed by a solicitor before relying on it, to ensure full compliance with UK GDPR and, where relevant, the EU GDPR.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy explains how Execution Premium LTD ("La Brélie", "we", "us", "our"), registered in England & Wales, company number 12713787, registered office 9 Ewald Road, London, SW6 3NA, collects and uses personal data through labrelie.com.

We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

2. What we collect

Currently, the only personal data we collect through this site is the email address you provide if you sign up to our newsletter. If the app or online store launch with accounts in future, this policy will be updated to reflect any additional data collected at that point.

3. How we use it

  • To send you our newsletter: news, app launch updates, recipe spotlights, and behind-the-scenes content.
  • We do not sell or rent your email address to third parties.

4. Legal basis

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the newsletter signup form. You can withdraw this consent at any time by unsubscribing (a link is included in every newsletter email) or by contacting us directly.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to the newsletter, or until you ask us to delete it.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe we have mishandled your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at arthur@delabrelie.com.

8. Cookies

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies of its own. The newsletter signup widget (Supascribe) and Netlify hosting may set minimal technical cookies required for them to function. If this changes — for example, if we add analytics in future — this policy will be updated.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to arthur@delabrelie.com.
